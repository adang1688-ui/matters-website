Privacy & Safety

Your words are yours.

We've tried to be straightforward about what we do and don't do with your data.

Encrypted in our database

Your entries, moods, and reflections are encrypted at rest in our database.

No selling

Your data is never sold to third parties. Your entries exist only for you.

Powered by Anthropic & OpenAI

AI reflections are generated by Anthropic's Claude. The text you send to Wren is sent to Anthropic to write a reply. If you use voice input, your speech is processed by OpenAI to create a text transcript. Matters does not save the audio.

Delete anytime

You can delete your entries or your entire account at any time. When you delete, it's removed from your account. Backups may keep it for a limited time.

Not a therapy service

Matters is a space for private reflection, not a clinical tool. If you're in crisis, please reach out to a professional. Resources are always available in the app.

Working toward more

We're a small team building this carefully. We'll be honest about where we are and transparent when our privacy protections improve.
