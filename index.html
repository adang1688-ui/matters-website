Questions

Everything you might
want to know.

Honest answers about what Matters is, how it works, and how your data is protected.

ChatGPT and Grok are built to answer questions and solve problems. Matters is built to make you feel heard. There's no search bar, no "how can I help you today", no advice unless you ask for it. The entire experience is designed around one thing — helping you release what's weighing on you and feel lighter. ChatGPT will try to fix your problem. Matters just holds it with you.

Matters is for anyone carrying something they haven't been able to say out loud. You don't have to be in crisis. You don't have to be a journaler. You just need a private space to put something down — stress, grief, anxiety, loneliness, or anything in between. If you've ever texted a friend just to vent, or stayed quiet because you didn't want to burden anyone — Matters is for you.

No. Matters is not therapy, does not provide clinical treatment, and is not a substitute for professional mental health support. Think of it as the space between "I'm fine" and calling a therapist — a private place to process what's on your mind. Wren will never diagnose, treat, or provide clinical recommendations. If you're in crisis, please reach out to 988 (call or text) or text HOME to 741741.

No. We have a strict no-access policy and will never read your personal entries. Your entries are securely stored and only accessible by you. We never sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for any purpose other than delivering the app experience to you. When you use Wren, your text is processed by Anthropic's API to generate a reflection. Anthropic does not store or train on your entries.

When you express something in Matters, Wren reads what you wrote and reflects it back — not to repeat it, but to help you see it from a slightly different angle. It acknowledges what you're feeling, offers a gentle reframe, and sometimes asks one question to help you go a little deeper — only if you want to. It won't give advice. It won't diagnose you. It won't push you anywhere you don't want to go.

Everything is deleted permanently. Your entries, your account, your emotional data — all of it. We don't keep backups of personal data after account deletion. You can delete your account at any time from the Profile tab inside the app.

Yes — Matters is completely free during our beta period. When we launch paid plans, everyone who joined during beta will be the first to know and will receive a special founding member offer.

Matters is not a crisis service. If you're in immediate danger or need urgent support, please reach out to: 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988), Crisis Text Line (text HOME to 741741), or Emergency services (call 911). You are not alone. Help is available right now.
