Safety & privacy

Your words stay yours.

Matters is built on the belief that what you carry privately deserves to stay private.

Private by design

Your entries are never sold, never used to train AI, and never shared without your consent.

Crisis-aware

Wren detects signs of distress and gently connects you to real human support — always.

Not therapy

Matters is a supportive space, not a clinical service. We're honest about what we are — and aren't.

Transparent AI
