Privacy & Safety

Your words are yours.

Who sees what I write?

Only you. Your entries are encrypted and private to your account, and we never sell your data. To write Wren's reply, your text is sent to an AI language model. Voice is turned into text by a speech model, and we don't save the audio.

Is this a replacement for therapy?

No. Wren is an AI guide, not a therapist or a crisis service.

What if I'm in crisis?

The Resources tab is always free, with crisis lines by country and an international directory. In the US or Canada, call or text 988. Wren points you to real support when it notices distress. Safety checks work in English today, with more languages to come.

Has a professional reviewed it?

Not yet. We'll say so here when that changes.

Can I delete it?

Yes, anytime. Delete your entries or your whole account. Backups may keep it for a short time.

Who is it for?

Adults 18 and older.

If you are in crisis or worried about your safety, please reach out to a real person. Matters is not a crisis service.

What you can do

Start anywhere. Here's the path.

Four things to try, in the order most people find useful. No setup, no pressure.

01
Express it

Say what's weighing on you. Type it, speak it, or write it in your journal. One word is enough to start. Voice works in English today, with more languages to come. Matters does not save the audio.

Try it: put down whatever is on your mind right now.

02
Reflect with Wren

Wren, our AI guide, answers with a short, gentle reflection on what you shared. Read it, or tap to hear it read aloud. Not advice, just a space to be heard.

Try it: send one sentence and see what Wren notices.

03
Try a calm exercise

Breathing, a body scan, or a grounding practice, for when you need to settle before you go on with your day.

Try it: pick one and notice how you feel afterward.

04
See what keeps coming up

Over time, Insights shows the patterns across your entries: your most common feelings, the themes, and the moments you carry the most weight. When something stands out, a calm exercise can help you find your balance.

Try it: check back after a few entries.

Check out Resources

Crisis lines and support services are always one tap away, and always free.
